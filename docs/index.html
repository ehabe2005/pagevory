Before the first page

Questions you might have

Do I need a finished idea?

No. A topic you know well and a sense of what your audience needs are enough. We’ll work out together whether there is a useful ebook to create.

What do I need to contribute?

Your expertise and content (for example recipes, methods or photos you own), feedback on the drafts and final approval. You also introduce the ebook to your audience, in whatever way suits you — there’s no minimum number of posts.

Is there really no upfront fee?

Yes. Instead of charging for writing and design, we share the net revenue 50/50. No other costs can be added without your written agreement.

Do I keep the rights to my content?

Yes. You keep ownership of your texts, photos, name and brand. You only grant a limited licence to use them in this ebook and its approved promotion.

Will I approve everything before it goes on sale?

Always. Nothing is published, priced or changed without your written approval of the PDF, the price and the product page.

Do you use AI tools?

We may use AI tools to support layout and editing, always with human review, and only with your written permission for any unpublished content. We never imitate your voice or image.

How do payments work?

We sell through Whop and set up its revenue-share feature, so your share of each sale goes straight to your Whop balance. You also get a monthly summary of sales and deductions.

Do you work internationally?

Yes. We are based in Italy and work in English and Spanish with creators around the world, as long as Whop supports payouts in your country.
